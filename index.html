Our services

From one fixture to the full show.

Whether you envision an extravagant light show or a soothing, atmospheric glow, our lighting specialists bring your ideas to life, on time and on budget.

LED Lighting Rentals

Your one-stop shop for LED lighting. High-quality equipment at an affordable rate, backed by experienced technicians who make sure your event looks its best.

Lighting Consoles & Control

Console and control rentals built on the latest technology, including grandMA3 and grandMA2 workstations, with expert assistance so your event runs smoothly.

Other Rental Equipment

Cable in every length, grip gear for film sets, truss, distro and road cases to protect it all: everything a production needs, from simple fixtures to complex rigs.

Lighting Design & Strategy

Custom lighting plans tailored to each client, created with you by experienced designers who bring innovation and efficiency to every space.

Custom Installations

Permanent set-ups for restaurants, night clubs and studios, or temporary rigs for corporate events, theatricals and Christmas. Our team is certified to operate heavy machinery when needed.

Lighting Programming

Show-stopping visuals programmed by our creative team, from custom lighting automation to timecoding, so your production truly stands out.

Events & installations

Lighting up Ontario’s holidays.

Holiday Fair in the Square

With the City of Toronto, at Nathan Phillips Square. The December 2022 fair ran with the Cavalcade of Lights: a Christmas market, winter carnival and light show in the heart of downtown.

Canadian Tire Christmas Trail

A drive-along experience for Canadian Tire’s 100th birthday: a 1.5-kilometre journey of thousands of twinkling lights, interactive displays and live entertainment.

Twinkle After Dark

A 1 km holiday walk-through with historic buildings, holiday projections, light tunnels and over 500,000 Christmas bulbs.

FromSanta.ca

Created by Sean Thompson in 2020, when kids couldn’t visit Santa at the mall: families booked FaceTime calls with Santa, over 1,500 in the first year, with all proceeds to SickKids.
